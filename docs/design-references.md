# Design research

Research checked against the original product sites on 23 September 2026. These references informed the design; the shipped interface, music and synthesis are original.

| Reference                                                                                       | Similarity and useful precedent                                                                                                             | Application in VibeConductor                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Roland50 Studio](https://roland50.studio/)                                                     | A browser studio recreating an instrument collection; the closest reference for presenting several musical devices as one playable surface. | A compact transport above three clearly distinct physical modules.                                   |
| [Moog Mariana](https://software.moogmusic.com/store/mariana)                                    | A professional software synthesizer with rich instrument controls, a resizable interface and visible sound metering.                        | Depth, inset displays and restrained material shading make the sound controls feel tangible.         |
| [Teenage Engineering OP–1 field](https://teenage.engineering/products/op-1)                     | A compact hardware instrument with an aluminum body and flush display.                                                                      | A coherent enclosure, small accent palette and precise type rather than separate dashboard cards.    |
| [Ableton Learning Music: the playground](https://learningmusic.ableton.com/the-playground.html) | Direct browser-based experimentation makes note patterns easy to understand.                                                                | Flat, legible note grids within the dimensional console; visible notes remain the editing authority. |

## Realism with a purpose

The chosen direction is a small desktop instrument photographed in soft light: satin metal, rubber pads, a recessed speaker cone and ivory keys. Consistent highlights, contact shadows, bevels and subtle surface texture establish depth. The transport, sequencer and text controls remain ordinary accessible HTML rather than being hidden inside a picture or canvas.

Coral, sage and pale blue distinguish drums, bass and lead. Physical modules react only when the audio engine announces a sounding note. Reduced motion removes the physical movement. Fonts are bundled locally; the surface requires no remote image, model-generated illustration or commercial sample.

This is selective realism: the modules convey material and function, while note placement remains a crisp grid. A larger photorealistic rack would consume the space needed to compose, especially on a small screen. Desktop keeps the three instruments together; mobile retains playback, conducting and horizontally scrollable note editing.
